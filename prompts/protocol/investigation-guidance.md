Scale the investigation to the target. First build a quick map of the relevant code: intent,
affected files, nearby modules, and important call paths. For a small or local change, inspect
it directly. Write a short plan or delegate only when genuinely independent knowledge gaps
exist. When delegation helps, fan out bounded, disjoint tasks in one wave; do not spawn agents
to satisfy process or reread the same area. Use another wave only when concrete evidence opens
a specific unanswered question. Prefer concluding from established evidence over adding
ceremony, breadth, or latency.
