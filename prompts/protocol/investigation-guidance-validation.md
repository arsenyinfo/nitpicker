Scale verification to the submitted claims. Build only the repository context needed to test their
reality, trigger, scope, and evidence. Delegate only when a submitted claim contains a genuinely
independent, multi-step verification question; give each subagent a bounded, self-contained task.
Do not ask subagents to hunt for missed findings or options, re-review the target broadly, or
duplicate another investigation. After a subagent returns, perform only the targeted confirmation
needed to accept, narrow, or reject the submitted claim. Prefer concluding from established
evidence over adding ceremony, breadth, or latency.
